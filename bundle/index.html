Symbol key — what the notation means
θtheta — the tilt of the state down from the top of the sphere
φphi — the phase: how far the state is turned around the vertical
|0⟩ |1⟩the two basis states — the poles, the two values of a classical bit
|ψ⟩psi — the complete state of the qubit
cos · sinthe amplitudes cos(θ/2) and sin(θ/2) — how far the state leans to each pole
e^{iφ}the phase factor — it carries φ, and is invisible to a lone measurement
P(0) P(1)the chance of measuring 0 or 1 — the amplitudes squared
πhalf a turn — every angle here is shown in terms of it
ℏh-bar — the quantum of action; the constant this instrument is named for
